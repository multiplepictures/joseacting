I am Jose Ramirez Hernandez an aspiring actor and filmmaker in Los Angeles, California. I began driving for Uber in 2016 and immediately was sent to Hollywood. After a few months of driving and daily networking, I picked up an Uber passenger that turned out to be a casting director and offered me my first acting job. I have been in short films, done theater and some background work. I quickly found out that it is very difficult to make a living as an actor. Eventually I began looking into being behind the camera, which is also just as difficult to make a living in. It has been a struggle but I have not given up yet. I occasionally work on a project now and then, but not as often as I should.

When I have free time

  1. I like to see and study my favorite films
  2. I try to learn something new or enhance existing skills
  3. I spend time with my parents and family

My favorite website is Actors Access because it has the lowest price to find paid casting calls.
